Key Takeaways Federal cybersecurity support for healthcare is shrinking fast. MS-ISAC, which supplied 90% of local threat intelligence for 21 years, lost federal funding on October 1, 2025, cutting off free threat data for more than 19,000 state, local and healthcare organizations. CISA has lost roughly a third of its workforce.
