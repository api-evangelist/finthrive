The Financial Pressure on Healthcare Has Reached a New Threshold Healthcare organizations are navigating a financial environment that looks fundamentally different than it did even a few years ago. Coverage is more volatile, patient responsibility continues to climb and margin pressure has become a constant rather than a cycle. Recent industry data shows hospital bad debt and charity care are up 10 percent in 2025 and 40 percent higher than in 2022 , reflecting sustained pressure on the front end of the revenue cycle.
