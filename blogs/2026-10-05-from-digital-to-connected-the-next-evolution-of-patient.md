Key Takeaways Healthcare organizations have made significant progress digitizing patient access, financial transparency and self-service experiences. The next evolution of patient engagement is creating continuity across those experiences by connecting the data, workflows and intelligence behind them. Organizations that deliver a more coordinated patient financial journey are better positioned to improve both patient experience and revenue cycle performance.
